The logo

Two lowercase l’s. No feet. No bull.

This is a typographic logo: the name itself, set in a confident, heavy typeface, with no symbol and no bull. Its one distinctive detail is the pair of lowercase l’s. Without feet, they sit closer together and tuck in tight beside the U, so the word forms a more compact, unique shape that’s recognisable at a glance and unlike any other insurance logo. It holds up in one colour, at small sizes and alongside partner logos.

The blinking cursor is an optional extra. Because the l’s already resemble a text cursor, the second one can blink and type, giving us a simple way to show what No Bull is and isn’t: the fine print is typed out, then deleted. It suits video and digital channels, but the logo stands on its own without it.

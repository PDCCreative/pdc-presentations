The logo

Every answer ends with a full stop.

A full stop marks the end of a clear answer, with no ifs or buts, and that’s the idea behind this logo. The full stop after NO BULL says ‘that’s final’. The logo works perfectly well as a static mark. When animated, the dot pops out of the O, bounces along Straight-Up Insurance and lands in place.

Two typefaces, three colourways

The current Inter wordmark and the Clash Display wordmark, stacked so each colourway can be compared directly across both typefaces.

Version A

Inter

Inter is a common Google Font: clear, familiar and easy to read at any size. We’ve tweaked some of the letterforms so the wordmark is our own, but the plain, everyday typeface is a deliberate choice. Nothing fancy and nothing hidden, so the type itself reflects the No Bull approach. It also works well at small sizes, on screen and beside partner logos.

Version B

Clash Display

Clash Display has more character. Its letterforms, also tweaked slightly, are squatter and sturdier, giving the wordmark a grounded stance and a bit more attitude. This version is led by personality: still straight-talking, but with more swagger, so No Bull stands apart from the safe, corporate look of most insurance brands.
